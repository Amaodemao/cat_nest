Research
  • Data Poisoning on Reasoning Large Language Models, University of California, Santa Barbara, Jun 2025 – Dec 2025
    • Proposed ItTakesTwo, a two-stage paradigm that improves the scalability and stealthiness of data poisoning attacks against large reasoning models while preserving model utility.
    • Evaluated attack success rate and auditability across various reasoning language models.
  • Summer Research Internship, CUHK CSE Dept., May 2023 – Sep 2023
    • BiasPainter: Fairness Analysis of AI Image Generators
      • Proposed BiasPainter, a metamorphic testing framework that can accurately, automatically and comprehensively trigger social bias in image generation models.
      • Built an image dataset and a prompt dataset based on publicly accessible data sources, and designed a set of metamorphic relations to evaluate the bias of image generation models.
      • Evaluated the bias of mainstream image generation models, including commercial models like Stable Diffusion XL and MidJourney.
  • Research Assistant, CUHK CSE Dept., Sep 2022 – Feb 2023
    • Bias Evaluation on Open-domain Question Answering Systems
      • Conducted experiments on triggering bias in question answering (QA) systems.
      • Designed a standardized algorithm to evaluate the bias of different QA models.
  • Research Assistant, CUHK CSE Dept., Feb 2022 – Aug 2022
    • Robotic Surgery Intelligence
